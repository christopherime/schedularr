Guide draft mode

A verdict is the diff against the committed reading: NEW, CHANGED, REMOVED, or SAME. Text always carries it — a chip on every changed slot, never color alone. A removed slot moves to the second lane beside the ghosts so it never gets painted over by what replaces it; SAME dims to keep the diff the point. Under the bar states are the three failures the draft zone can show.
